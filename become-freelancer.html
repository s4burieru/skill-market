Questions freelancers ask

Anything else? Our support team replies within hours.

How much can I earn on SkillMarket?

It depends on your skill and experience. New freelancers typically start with smaller projects to build reviews, then raise their rate. Our current top-rated sellers charge between ₱2,600 and ₱6,800 per hour.

When do I get paid?

Clients fund each milestone before you start it. Once the work is approved, the funds are released and become available for withdrawal within two business days.

Do I need to pay to join?

No. Creating a profile, publishing a service and sending proposals are free. We only take a 10% service fee when you actually get paid, and it drops to 5% for repeat clients.

Can I work with clients outside my country?

Yes — SkillMarket is used in 120 countries. Contracts, payments and currency conversion all happen through the platform, and support is available in four languages.

What happens if a project goes wrong?

Open a dispute and our mediation team reviews the milestones, deliverables and message history. Funds held in escrow are distributed based on that review, so neither side is left guessing.
