Reviewer notes

What good reviewers check first

What do I check before approving a service?

Delivery scope clarity, price vs. market, seller response time, and whether the samples match the claim. Approve the work, not the person.

When do I escalate to Trust & Safety?

Payment disputes, harassment, identity fraud, or anything with a real-world safety dimension. When unsure, escalate, reviewers never get in trouble for escalating.

Can a suspension be undone?

Yes. Go to Users → suspended accounts → Reinstate. The original moderator is notified automatically, and reinstatement is logged to the audit trail.
