Questions clients ask

Anything else? Our support team replies within hours.

How do I post a job?

Create a client account, describe the work you need done, set a budget and a deadline, and publish. Posting is free and matched proposals usually start arriving the same day.

What does it cost to hire?

Posting is free. You pay a 5% hiring fee only on the escrow you release, plus whatever you agree to pay the freelancer. Repeat hires drop the fee to 3%, and there are no subscriptions or listing charges.

How does escrow protect my money?

When you approve a milestone, the funds are reserved before the work starts. They are released only once you accept the deliverable, and if the work never arrives, the escrow is refunded to you.

Can I hire people in other countries?

Yes. Freelancers on SkillMarket work from 120 countries. Contracts, messaging, invoicing and currency conversion all run through the platform, and support is available in four languages.

What if the work is not what I asked for?

Request revisions in the milestone, or open a dispute. Our mediation team reviews the brief, the deliverables and the message history, then decides how the held escrow is distributed.
