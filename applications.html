Writing a better brief

Briefs that pull 2× the applicants

What should the first paragraph say?

What you are building, why now, and what "done" looks like. Candidates skip briefs that lead with company history instead of the problem.

How much budget should I put?

Always a real range, even a rough one. "Confidential" budgets get roughly half the applications of transparent ones.

How many skills should I tag?

Three to five. More than that and matching dilutes; fewer and good candidates filter you out entirely.
