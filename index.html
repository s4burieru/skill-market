You approve a freelancer’s offer and fund the milestone into escrow before work begins. SkillMarket holds the money and releases it automatically when you approve the milestone: no invoices, no chasing, no surprises.

Posting a brief and joining as a freelancer are always free. Clients pay the agreed price plus a small payment-processing fee at checkout. Freelancers pay a flat 10% service fee, deducted only when a milestone is released. Premium adds boosts and verification badges, optional, never required.

Every week we recalculate rankings within each category from star rating, completed projects and on-time delivery. Category leaders wear a crown, and it resets whenever performance slips, so the top spot always reflects current work.

Most briefs receive their first proposals within 24 hours. A clear scope, a realistic budget and example links move the needle the most. In a hurry? Mark the brief urgent and we’ll nudge the best-matched freelancers directly.

Start with revisions: most services include them. If you and the freelancer still can’t agree, open a dispute before approving the milestone: the escrow stays locked while our team reviews the contract, messages and deliverables, then releases payment as it was earned.
