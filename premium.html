Questions about ranking & verification

Everything else, just email support, we reply within hours.

What does the crown mean?

Every category crowns its own top three each week. The top-ranked freelancer in each category wears the gold crown, second place gets silver and third gets bronze. The crown shows on their cards, their profile and in the weekly digest, and disappears as soon as someone else outranks them in that category.

Is verification really free?

Yes, forever. Verification exists to make clients safer, SkillMarket covers the cost of the ID check and the portfolio review. We only earn when you earn, which is via the service fee on completed projects.

How is my score calculated?

Score = rating × 14 + min(reviews, 150) × 0.12 + min(completed, 150) × 0.08, capped at 100. Rating is worth up to 70 points. Review and project volume are capped so a long grind can’t beat a 5.0 rating, and every category board re-ranks every Monday.

Can I lose Premium or verification?

Verified status can only be revoked for violations, fraud, fake reviews or identity mismatch. Premium is month-to-month: cancel anytime and the badge disappears at the end of the paid period. Nothing else changes about your profile.

Does Premium inflate my rating?

Never. Reviews come from completed contracts and are moderated independently. Premium buys placement and the lower 5% fee, it cannot buy, hide or remove reviews.
